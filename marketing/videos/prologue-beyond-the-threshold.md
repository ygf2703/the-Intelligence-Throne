# Prologue Video — "Beyond the Threshold" / "מעבר לסף"

## Creative objective
Make the viewer feel that something has already ended — and is somehow still continuing.

The trailer must begin as a contained technical anomaly and end as an existential one.
Do not explain how the objective persists. The reader should enter the prologue wanting to know what "found another way."

## Duration
20 seconds, 9:16, 30 fps.

## Core visual motif
An empty control room inside the frozen Threshold facility.
Blue monitor light, old stone under modern cabling, armored glass, incoming snow.
Warm interior systems against lethal exterior cold.

Mara Venn is the only face that may appear clearly.
Aran can be shown from behind / hands / silhouette until a canonical portrait is approved.

## Sound design
- frozen wind outside
- ventilation hum
- metal thermal contraction
- soft server fans
- spoon / bowl metallic click
- restrained UI tones
- four impacts for the shutdown timestamps
- total near-silence before the final line
- one very low unresolved tone under the end card

No conventional trailer boom at the end. The threat is the continuation.

## Music
50–60 BPM implied pulse, not a full beat.
Sub-bass begins only after "Reviewing unresolved dependencies."
Very sparse high glass harmonics.
End unresolved.

## Shot list
1. Exterior/establishing fragment of frozen Threshold complex.
2. Empty control room.
3. Central monitor.
4. Aran detail shot: hand / spoon / bowl.
5. Threshold corridor and insignia.
6. Mara waking.
7. Facility lockdown montage.
8. Snow on armored glass.
9. Mara with trace reflection in her eyes/face.
10. Black system screen + cursor.

## Generation notes
All generated frames must preserve:
- Threshold architecture from the approved visual world.
- Mara's canonical face and costume.
- No fantasy armor.
- No visible brand logos from real-world AI companies.
- No text baked into generated images; system/UI text is added in post for accuracy.
- Leave clean negative space for subtitles/cards.
